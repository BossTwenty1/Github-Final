Archival Biography & In Memoriam Notes

Evelyn served as an emergency civilian triage nurse during World War II in the Pacific Northwest staging hospitals before serving 34 years as Head of Pediatric Nursing at Providence Hospital. Preceded in death by her spouse of 54 years, Kenneth Vance Harper (interned adjacent, Plot A-401). Beloved grandmother of seven and great-grandmother of four.
